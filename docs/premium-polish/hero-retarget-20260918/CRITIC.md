# Independent visual review — two actual keyframe trials

Reviewer: independent stock_motion_critic agent; no source modifications or engine runs.

## Attempt 2 — eight actual native stills inspected

Walk_Carry is provisionally promising. Do not render a full clip of TreeChopping
unchanged. Frames 003, 006 and 007 hide essentially the entire tool and both hands
behind the character. Frames 004–005 show the tool low beside the boot/hip, with
little wind-up versus strike contrast. Contact is insufficiently visible to
verify convincing two-handed grip. No obvious gross leg distortion; one lifted
walking sole is readable. Timing, smoothness, weight, sliding and stopping cannot
be established from these stills. Swing-orientation mismatch was then a hypothesis.

## Attempt 3 — chopping frames 003, 004, 005, 006 inspected

Reject this source/adaptation as a shortcut for two-handed mining; a full movie
is not justified. Frames 003–004 bunch the hands near the head; 005 hides almost
the entire tool; 006 exposes part above the shoulder. There is still no clear
wind-up and outward two-handed strike. The supplied actual one-handed source
measurements explain the compatibility problem; a correction exceeding the native
arm length substantially replaces the intended trajectory. This conclusion is
limited to the particular source/adaptation, not the hero or CC0/mocap generally.
