# Independent camera regression check

No concrete regression found in the reviewed 667-width landscape states. Actual DOWN→UP captures were inspected in Mossvein, Rootwound Depth2, Deepheart and the normal entrance to The Deep. Each candidate frame keeps the whole hero visible, retains the real world camera limits and player coordinates, and correctly reverses vertical lookahead. The Surface DOWN and UP framing metrics are exactly equal between the original DEV15.55 package and the camera-only candidate: camera position/centre, zoom, native hero frame, world limits and framing snapshot. No new hero/minimap overlap was measured.
